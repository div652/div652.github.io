I am an ML Engineer & Technical Lead at Samsung Electronics (VD Division - AI & Big Data Lab) in Suwon-si, South Korea. I lead cross-office projects across Delhi, Nanjing, and Seoul, focusing on scalable on-device AI, computer vision, and IoT solutions. I am deeply passionate about pushing the boundaries of research in computer science.

### Education & Academic Excellence
I hold a B.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering from the **Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Delhi** (2020 - 2024), where I graduated with an exceptional GPA of **9.76/10**. I was awarded the IIT Delhi Merit Prize and the Endowment Merit Scholarship for being in the top 7% of my class. During Fall 2022, I studied as an exchange student at the University of Waterloo, ranking 1st among over 1200 selected peers. Before university, I secured All India Rank 3 in JEE Mains (100 percentile) and AIR 97 in JEE Advanced.

### Professional & Research Experience
- **Samsung Electronics (HQ, South Korea)** *(Sep 2024 - Present)*: As a Technical Lead, I manage sensor capability APIs for SmartThings TV IoT, serving 200M+ users globally. I built a Camera IoT service with 30-fps live-streaming and on-device object detection, optimized MobileNet-v1 via quantization, and improved Sentence Keyword Extraction using knowledge distillation. During my internship here *(Summer 2023)*, I developed an LSTM+Attention Exercise Recognition model with 92% accuracy. Currently, my work heavily involves the Extended Reality (XR) framework team, providing scalable AI solutions utilizing LLMs as APIs for platform developers.
- **Undergraduate Research & Mentorship**: I worked with Prof. Parag Singla on a Neuro-Symbolic Concept Learner (NSCL), learning generalizable concepts from few-shot demonstrations, leading to an ICRA 2026 submission. I also researched under Prof. Ashish Chiplunkar & Prof. Rohit Vaish on Prophet Inequalities, improving the SOTA guarantee for semi-online multi-selection algorithms. Additionally, I served as a **Teaching Assistant** for *Introduction to Computer Science (COL100)* for an entire year (Jan '24 - Dec '24), mentoring and grading over 600 students, and earlier acted as an Academic Mentor to incoming freshmen.
- **University of Wisconsin-Madison** *(Summer 2022)*: As a research intern in the HAL Research Group, I profiled the Gem5 system-level simulator, identifying and rectifying simulation inaccuracies in GPU cache behavior.
