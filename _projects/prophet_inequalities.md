- Analysed various academic texts on prophet problems to understand current bounds on the competitive ratio.
- Used the novel approach of shortlisting in the iid prophet problem to improve the Gambler's expected payoff.
- Improved the SOTA guarantee for the semi-online multi-selection prophet algorithm from $1-\frac{1}{2e}$ to $1-o(1)$.
- Constructed the hard instance of a novel 3-point distribution, establishing an upper bound of 0.90 and tightening limits for semi-online selection.
- Project shortlisted for the Summer Undergraduate Research Award by Industrial Research & Development Unit, IIT Delhi.
